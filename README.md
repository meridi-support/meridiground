# 🪨 MeridiGround

**From your doorstep up to space and down to Earth's core — a free, ad-free explorer in your browser.**
足もとから、空の上へも、地球の中心へも。広告なし・登録なしの、陸の図鑑。

👉 **[Open MeridiGround](https://meridibrand.com/ground/)**

## What's inside / できること
| Section | Contents |
|---|---|
| ⬆ Up to the sky / 空へ | 16 stops — Tokyo Tower & Eiffel Tower, Skytree, Burj Khalifa, Coors Field ⚾, Estadio Azteca ⚽, Fuji, Kilimanjaro, Everest, the highest-flying bird, airliners, ozone layer, Kármán line, ISS → 🌌 MeridiStars |
| 🏠 0 m | A Showa-era house, building & mountain silhouettes to scale, links to 🌊 MeridiDepths |
| ⬇ Down to the core / 地底へ | Subway stations, Roppongi (42.3 m), the "underground temple" flood channel, hot springs, the deepest mine, the Kola borehole, crust, mantle, core |
| 🕳 Caves / 洞窟 | Lava tubes vs limestone caves (Narusawa, Fugaku, Akiyoshidō, Ryūsendō, Mammoth Cave, Sơn Đoòng, Waitomo) |
| ⏳ Time / 時間 | 4.6-billion-year calendar slider, the Chibanian Age, fossils |
| 💎 Rocks / 岩石と鉱物 | Rock cycle, everyday stones, jade, Mohs scale & quiz |
| 🌋 Volcanoes & hot springs / 火山と温泉 | Four volcano shapes, geysers, famous onsen, ten spring types |
| 🧩 Plates / プレート | Three boundary types & hot spots, the interactive ruler experiment, Japan's four plates, world plate map (touch a line for plate names) |
| 🌍 World stages / 世界の舞台 | 11 places with animated cross-sections — Japan, Iceland, Yellowstone, Hawaii, Himalayas, San Andreas, Colorado Plateau, Uyuni, Death Valley, Dead Sea, East African Rift |
| 🌐 Equator / 赤道 | Earth's bulge, Chimborazo, northern vs southern hemisphere |
| 🌏 Cross-section / 断面図 | Earth's five layers with your current depth |
| 📖 Guide / 図鑑 | ~130 entries, 9 categories, search, tap to jump |

Plus a side scale, live gauge (altitude/depth, temperature, layer), 📍 Google Maps links, m/km ⇔ ft/mi, and Japanese / English.

## Notes / ご注意
- Figures are approximate. Records and buildings under construction may change.
  数字は「だいたい」です。記録や建設中の建物は変わることがあります。
- This page does not show live earthquake or volcano information. / 今起きている地震や火山の情報ではありません。

## Privacy
No ads, no sign-up, no tracking cookies. Everything runs in your browser. We only count page views with [GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com/).
